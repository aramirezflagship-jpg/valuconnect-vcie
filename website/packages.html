How Payment Works

Secure & Straightforward

Your investment is protected every step of the way. No payment before we agree on the scope.

1

Free Assessment

Book via our website or reach out on social media. A free 30-minute call where we map exactly where your business is losing time — no tech jargon, no pressure.

2

Proposal & Contract

You receive a written proposal with exact deliverables, pricing, and a clear 30-day project timeline. We send a simple service contract — once signed, we're officially partners.

3

50% Deposit & Invoice

Pay 50% upfront via Stripe (card, bank transfer, or ACH). An invoice is automatically sent to your email the moment payment clears. Work begins the same day.

4

Setup, Training & Go-Live

We build your digital systems, migrate your existing data, and run hands-on training until your team is confident. The final 50% is due only after you've approved the setup and completed training — then a final invoice is sent automatically. Monthly retainer begins if you choose one.
