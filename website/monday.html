FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a monday.com subscription before I hire you? ▼
No — you don't need anything before the discovery call. After we agree on scope, we'll help you select the right monday.com plan for your team size. monday.com starts at $9/user/month. That's a separate cost from our implementation fee, which covers our labor and expertise.
How long does the full setup take? ▼
Quick Start is delivered in 5 business days. Operations Essentials takes 7–10 days. Growth Suite takes 2–3 weeks. Custom Build is scoped to your project — typically 3–6 weeks. We give you a firm timeline in the proposal before any money changes hands.
What if I already have a monday.com account with some boards set up? ▼
No problem. We start with an audit of what you have, keep what's working, and rebuild what isn't. We won't charge you to start from scratch if you have a solid foundation already. The discovery call is where we assess this — it's free and there's no commitment.
Is the training really in Spanish? ▼
Yes. Andres Ramirez is bilingual and conducts all training in the language your team is most comfortable with — English, Spanish, or a mix of both. All written documentation and SOP guides can be delivered in both languages.
What's the 30-day setup guarantee? ▼
If your workspace isn't fully built, tested, and your team isn't trained within the agreed timeline, you don't pay the final 50%. We finish what we promised — or we refund the difference. The guarantee also covers the first 30 days of use: if something breaks due to our configuration, we fix it at no additional cost.
Do I need technical skills to maintain it after setup? ▼
No. We build systems that non-technical owners can manage day-to-day. Adding items, updating statuses, checking dashboards — all straightforward. If you want to build new automations or integrations down the road, that's what the monthly maintenance add-on is for.
